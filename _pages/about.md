I am a Ph.D. candidate at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), advised by [Prof. Gao Huang](http://www.gaohuang.net/) at [LeapLab](https://www.leaplab.ai/). I received my B.Eng. from Tsinghua University in 2023. I am currently a Top Seed Intern at ByteDance Seed.

My research centers on efficient and scalable architecture design, with a focus on test-time training, sequence modeling, context scaling, and continual learning. My work [ViT<sup>3</sup>: Unlocking Test-Time Training in Vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01643) was selected as a **CVPR 2026 Best Paper Finalist** (15 / 16,092 submissions) and an Oral presentation.
