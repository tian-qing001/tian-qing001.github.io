Dongchen Han

Tsinghua University

Beijing, China

hdc23@mails.tsinghua.edu.cn

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Gao Huang at LeapLab. I received my B.Eng. from Tsinghua University in 2023. I am currently a Top Seed Intern at ByteDance Seed.

My research centers on efficient and scalable architecture design, with a focus on test-time training, sequence modeling, context scaling, and continual learning. My work ViT3: Unlocking Test-Time Training in Vision was selected as a CVPR 2026 Best Paper Finalist (15 / 16,092 submissions) and an Oral presentation.
